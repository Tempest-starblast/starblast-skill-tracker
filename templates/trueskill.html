TrueSkill ratingBETA

An experimental rating being trialled to replace the current one.

This board is scored a different way. Instead of a late snapshot of a match, it watches every game from the opening and learns each player's skill purely from who beats whom — the rating is the same model that predicts who wins. It starts everyone from zero and runs alongside the normal leaderboard while we test it. Rating is a conservative estimate (it rises as the system grows more sure of you); ± is how unsure it still is; a SETTLING tag means fewer than 10 games so far. Think it's got you wrong?
