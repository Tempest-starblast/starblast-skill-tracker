Odyssey custom lobby

A custom team-mode lobby for top players, opened by the host on demand. The join link shows here for every Odyssey player (rank Odyssey or above) the moment one goes live. The host sets who can join: Odyssey only — and anyone the lobby doesn’t recognise as Odyssey is removed automatically — or anyone with the link. Left at its defaults it’s the standard rated game; change the settings, draw a map or import a mod and it’s an unrated custom game.
